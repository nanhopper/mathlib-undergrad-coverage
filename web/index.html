      Methods & sources

      What counts as coverage

      Covered means a recorded declaration or internal module reference. The named-theorem repository scope can include Archive and Counterexamples. A reference is not an independent audit of every proof or the full scope of a syllabus topic. Missing references do not establish absence from mathlib. The benchmarks have separate denominators; there is no combined score.

      External references, reports elsewhere or without a located repository reference, statement-only records, qualified records, and unlinked entries remain separate. An author name or a reported date is not sufficient to count a repository reference.

      Subject labels for named theorems use one pinned MSC taxonomy across history. That does not claim those were the historical classifications. The denominator remains the mathlib mirror, with unmatched entries retained rather than silently dropped.

      How comparisons work

      All cutoffs are in UTC. The observation cutoff, the source file’s commit timestamp, the upstream source head, and the data generation time are different dates. Monthly snapshots are observations of a curated catalog; a monthly cutoff excludes events exactly at that instant, while the latest observation includes its cutoff instant. The latest observation can also include a partial month.

      The 3-, 6-, and 12-month comparisons use adjacent complete-calendar-month windows and the same identities continuously present throughout both. Excluded counts include historical identities seen anywhere during the paired period but outside the common cohort, not just entries in the selected observation’s catalog. Missing history or a missing common subject cohort is unavailable, never zero. The displayed boundaries come from the data’s closed window, not from the date of the latest snapshot. Intervals include their start cutoff and exclude their end cutoff: 1 March to 1 September covers March through August.

      Same-cohort net change is reference gains minus losses. Net entries per month divides that count by the number of calendar months. Percentage-point change divides the net count by the common cohort, not by today’s changing catalog size.

      Full-catalog change also includes referenced additions and removals. Renames and reference-format edits are not automatically gains. Initial catalog population is a baseline; documentation batches and repository-port boundaries are not proof-production bursts. Recording can arrive long after formalization, so recording dates cannot identify when a proof was completed.

      Historical evidence is replayed through the chosen observation. Declaration names and relative module paths are shown as recorded, alongside a source YAML link pinned to that revision. We do not guess declaration URLs or silently substitute current evidence. External reference destinations may have changed since they were recorded. Reported dates retain their original precision.
